# Voice of Kannada — Hackathon Live Demo Script

**Total Demo Duration**: 3 to 4 minutes  
**Goal**: Demonstrate real Kannada speech recognition, real AI dialogue, Kannada speech synthesis, structured learning, and transparent speaking evaluation to judges.

---

### Step 1: Introduction & Dashboard (30 seconds)
1. Open the application. Highlight the deep navy and emerald design, clean typography (Noto Sans Kannada), and zero-pill layout discipline.
2. Point out the **Phrase of the Day**: *"ಕನ್ನಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಮಾತನಾಡಿ, ಕನ್ನಡ ಉಳಿಸಿ"*.
3. Click **"Listen Pronunciation"** to demonstrate immediate audio playback.
4. Show the everyday Karnataka scenarios (Auto ride, Darshini coffee, Metro).

---

### Step 2: AI Voice Assistant (90 seconds)
1. Click **"AI Voice Assistant"** in the sidebar.
2. Click or hold the large central microphone button:
   - Say: *"ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ, ನೀವು ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀರಿ?"*
   - Show the animated green ripple rings and the **Audio Visualizer** waving in real-time.
   - Point out the live interim speech transcript updating progressively.
3. Observe the structured response:
   - **Kannada Script**: *"ನಾನು ತುಂಬಾ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದ್ದೇನೆ, ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು!..."*
   - **Roman Transliteration**: *"Nānu tumbā cennāgiddēne..."*
   - **English Meaning**: *"I am doing very well, thank you!..."*
   - **Grammar Note**: Breakdown of key terms.
4. Note that audio automatically plays back in Kannada. Demonstrate the **"Stop Audio"** button in the header.
5. Click one of the **Suggested Replies** at the bottom to show prompt continuation.
6. Click the **Bookmark** icon to save the phrase to your Vocabulary Notebook.

---

### Step 3: Kannada Learning Studio (45 seconds)
1. Navigate to **"Learning Studio"**.
2. Filter modules by category: click **"Food & Dining"**.
3. Open **"ಊಟ ಮತ್ತು ಉಪಹಾರ"** (Food & Dining Out).
4. Review the phrase *"ನನಗೆ ಒಂದು ಮಸಾಲೆ ದೋಸೆ ಬೇಕು"* (I want one masala dosa).
5. Inspect the word-by-word anatomical breakdown:
   - `ನನಗೆ` (nanage) = to me
   - `ಒಂದು` (ondu) = one
   - `ಮಸಾಲೆ ದೋಸೆ` (masāle dōse) = masala dosa
   - `ಬೇಕು` (bēku) = want
6. Click **"Complete This Module"**. Notice the progress bar increments instantly.

---

### Step 4: Speaking Practice Studio (60 seconds)
1. Navigate to **"Speaking Practice"**.
2. Listen to the target reference: *"ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ, ನೀವು ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀರಿ?"*.
3. Click the microphone button and pronounce the phrase.
4. Show the **Side-by-Side Grounded Comparison**:
   - Target phrase vs Recognized speech transcript.
   - Green token badges for matched words: `[✓] ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ`, `[✓] ನೀವು`, `[✓] ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀರಿ`.
   - Explain the honest mathematical alignment metric (no fabricated AI scores).
5. Demonstrate with an intentional partial omission to show the warning highlighting missing words.

---

### Step 5: Verification of Real Progress & Settings (30 seconds)
1. Open **"Progress & Mastery"**. Show that the completed module and the practice attempts appear in the real event log.
2. Open **"Settings & Privacy"**. Highlight the transparent microphone security disclosure and ability to reset all data.
3. Conclude by displaying the **Requirements Traceability Matrix** in the "About" page.
